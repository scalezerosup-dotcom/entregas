How to get started

  1. Open the explorer and find the place from the story you are reading right now.
  2. Download the part of the atlas that matches your reading (for example, Part 7 for the Gospels).
  3. Use Bonus 5 — 30 Days with the Bible — as a simple starter plan.
  4. For classroom or group use, print the Bible Quiz Workbook (Bonus 6).

If a download doesn’t start, tap “Open” and then save the file from your browser.
